## 2026-11-12
- **The changelog moved out of `research/README.md`** into this folder, one file per month.
- **Every `skeleton.cpp` function is named and typed** (bw1 30, bw2 28, bw3 29 were `FUN_`;
  bw3's unreferenced `RET` at `00550580` is padding and stays unnamed):

    (`clearModelAndSkeletonInUseFlags`, `markModelInUse`, `freeUnusedModelsAndSkeletons`), driven
    by `CDemonMission_prepareAllActors` and `CCharacter_markResourcesInUse`.
  - Every offset read agrees with the recorded `CSkeleton`, `CDeformableModel` and
    `CDeformableModelInstance` layouts.
- **Every `fire.cpp` function is named and typed** (bw1 19, bw2 22, bw3 52 entries):
  - **`CFireEffect_traceLaser` takes `SLaserInfo` by value** in every BW build: the recursive call
    copies 0x54 bytes onto the stack (`SUB ESP,0x54` / `REP MOVSD`, bw1 `004b69c1`).
  - **bw3 adds trailing arguments**, each stored to a field the layout already has: an explosion
    type (`CExplosion_activate`, `createExplosion`, `getExplosionEffect`), a decal type
    (`CBulletHole_init`, `createBulletImpact`) and the lightning bolt's mode, lifetime and alpha.
  - **bw3-only:** toss creators for BindEvil, Disease and Storm (toss types 1–3), five preload
    functions, `loadLightningBoltAssets(int bolt_style)`, and a storm effect: `CStormEffect`, 12
    bytes in a pool of 4 (`bw_own_structs.json`), which sets weather 5 for 5 seconds with
    `wp_storm.wav` and restores the previous weather. The name `CStorm` is the weapon's.
  - bw3's `0049e170` was misnamed `loadLightningBoltAssets`; it is `loadGunFlameAssets`. bw3's
    `CGunFlame_burnNearby` keeps its name, though it sits in nocedit's `CGunFlame_init` slot: it
    damages nearby characters.
